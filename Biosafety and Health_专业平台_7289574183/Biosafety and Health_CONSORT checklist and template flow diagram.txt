The SPIRIT (Standard Protocol Items: Recommendations for Interventional Trials) Statement is a minimum set of recommendations for reporting the protocol of a randomised trial. The recommendations are outlined in a 34-item checklist and a figure. We strongly recommend using the SPIRIT Statement in conjunction with the “Explanation and Elaboration” document, which explains and illustrates the principles underlying the SPIRIT Statement.

CONSORT 2025
The CONSORT (CONsolidated Standards Of Reporting Trials) Statement is an evidence-based, minimum set of recommendations for reporting the results of randomised trials. The recommendations are outlined in a 30-item checklist and a flow diagram. The checklist items focus on reporting how the trial was designed, analysed, and interpreted; the flow diagram displays the progress of all participants through the trial. We strongly recommend using the CONSORT Statement in conjunction with the “Explanation and Elaboration” document, which explains and illustrates the principles underlying the CONSORT Statement.
