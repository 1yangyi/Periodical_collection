一、期刊简介：
《有色金属（中英文）》（2025年—），即《Nonferrous Metals》 ，（原名《有色金属》（1949年），《有色金属工程》（2010—2024年），ISSN 2097-5384， CN 10-2019∕TF），1949年创刊，是由中国有色金属工业协会主管、矿冶科技集团有限公司（以下简称“矿冶集团”）主办、矿冶集团信息研究中心出版、面向国内外公开发行的权威学术期刊，主编为沈政昌院士。《有色金属（中英文）》的办刊宗旨为关注有色金属行业最新技术研发动态，搭建行业科技成果转化交流平台，推动行业科技创新和自主创新为建设资源节约型和环境友好型和谐社会服务。期刊学术氛围浓厚，重视科学前沿研究，是北大中文核心期刊、中国科技核心期刊，同时被美国《化学文摘》（CAS）、美国《史蒂芬斯全文数据库》(EBSCOhost)、日本《科学技术文献速报》（JICST)、日本《科学技术振兴机构（中国）文献数据库》（JSTChina）、《中国核心期刊（遴选）数据库》、《中国期刊全文数据库》(光盘版)、《万方数据》（CHINAINFO）、《维普资讯》（VIP INFORMATION）等国内外多家权威数据库机构收录。
二、征稿领域
文章为具有原创性和前瞻性、未公开发表的学术研究论文，选题新颖，观点鲜明，资料翔实、准确、说服力强，要求具有较强的学术价值和交流价值，或反映所研究领域的先进水平及发展趋势；或反映最新科研成果的研究进展与实施情况。
征稿领域为有色金属（轻、重、稀、贵）材料、采矿、选矿、冶炼、环保等领域方向，重点报道有色金属新材料、智能矿山、绿色矿冶、环境保护等学术进展，尤其对先进材料等热点方向重点关注。
三、格式规范
中文文章：需包含中英文对照的标题、基金项目、第一作者和通信作者简介、摘要（研究目的、方法、结果、结论）、关键词、正文（引言、实验部分、结果与讨论、结论）、参考文献等部分。
英文文章：需包含英文标题、第一作者和通信作者简介、摘要（研究目的、方法、结果、结论）、关键词、正文（引言、实验部分、结果与讨论、结论）、参考文献等部分。
语言质量：英文表达准确、流畅，避免语法和拼写错误。文章应尽量避免使用过于复杂的句子结构和生僻词汇，确保清晰传达研究内容。

Call for Papers for Nonferrous Metals
1  Journal Introduction
Nonferrous Metals (2025—), (formerly known as Nonferrous Metals (1949—2010) and Nonferrous Metals Engineering (2010—2024), ISSN 2095-1744, CN 10-1004/TF, postal code 80-371) was founded in 1949. It is an authoritative academic journal supervised by the China Nonferrous Metals Industry Association, sponsored by BGRIMM Technology Group, published by the Information Research Center of BGRIMM Technology Group, and publicly distributed domestically and internationally. The editor in chief is Academician Shen Zhengchang. The purpose of the publication of "Nonferrous Metals" is to focus on the latest technological research and development trends in the non-ferrous metal industry, build a platform for the transformation and exchange of industry scientific and technological achievements, and promote industry scientific and technological innovation and independent innovation to support the construction of a resource-saving and environmentally friendly harmonious society. The journal has a strong academic atmosphere and places great emphasis on cutting-edge scientific research. It is a core Chinese language journal of Peking University and a core science and technology journal of China. It is also included in authoritative domestic and international databases, such as Chemical Abstracts (CAS), EBSCOhost, JICST, JSTChina, Selected Chinese Core Journals, VIP Information.
2  Submission field
The article should be an original and forward-looking academic research paper that has not been publicly published. It should feature a novel topic, clear viewpoints, and be well-supported by accurate and substantial data, demonstrating strong persuasive power. The article should demonstrate strong academic and communication value, or reflect the advanced level and development trend of the research field, or showcase the research progress and implementation status of the latest scientific research.
In the fields of non-ferrous metals (light metals, heavy metals, rare metals, precious metals) materials, mining, beneficiation, smelting, environmental protection, etc., we focus on reporting academic progress in non-ferrous metal new materials, intelligent mining, green mining and metallurgy, environmental protection, and pay special attention to hot topics such as advanced materials.
3  Format specification
The Chinese article should include the following sections: bilingual title, funding project, introductions of the first author and corresponding author, abstract (research purpose, methods, results, conclusion), key words, main text (introduction, experimental section, results and discussion, conclusion), references, and other relevant sections.
The English article should include English title, introductions of the first author and corresponding author, abstract (research purpose, methods, results, conclusion), key words, main text (introduction, experimental section, results and discussion, conclusion), references, and other relevant sections.
Language quality: Accurate and fluent English expression, avoiding grammar and spelling errors. The article should avoid using overly complex sentence structures and obscure vocabulary to ensure clear communication of the research content.
