Author Guidelines
Article Types
Research Articles: original research work or a comprehensive and in-depth analysis of a topic (around 5,000 to 8,000 words).
Review Articles: manuscripts that provide a novel synthesis of a research area (around 5,000 to 10,000 words).
Research Notes: short data-based discussions of research findings of interest to the wider community (maximum 1,500 words, with at most one figure or one table, and no more than five references).
Perspectives: forward-looking viewpoints that advocate important future directions in the field (around 2,000 to 3,000 words).
Commentaries: call attention to published articles, books, or reports (around 1,000 to 2,000 words).
Letters to the Editor: comments on previously published articles in JDIS (around 1,000 to 2,000 words).
Opinions: pieces to present ideas, discuss recent books, propose arguments, or initiate debates (around 1,000 to 3,000 words).
Data Papers: articles that describe datasets, data collections, or metadata repositories, providing their scope, collection and curation methods, and information on accessibility, licensing, and citation in line with FAIR principles (https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/) (around 1,000 to 3,000 words).
Editorial: editors or guest editors may occasionally provide background information on an issue or an event.
Submission Guidelines
JDIS does not require specific formats for first submissions. Both Word and LaTeX file are acceptable file types. However, the following elements should be incorporated into your manuscript:
Names and affiliations of all authors.
For a research article, the abstract should be prepared in a structured form, including Purpose, Design/methodology/approach, Findings, Research limitations, Practical implications, and Originality/value.
Provide a list of up to six keywords.
Pages, sections, and subsections should be numbered. In addition, we suggest line numbering as well.
Figures and tables should be placed close to where they are first referenced.
JDIS strongly discourages the use of maps in submitted manuscripts. Authors are encouraged to employ alternative forms of visual representation, such as charts, graphs, or diagrams, to convey geographic or spatial information. If the inclusion of a map is deemed essential to the presentation of the research, authors are required to use the official map template provided by the JDIS Editorial Office.
References should adhere to Chicago Manual of Stylestyle (CMS author & date).
Acknowledgments, Author contribution statements (described using the standard NISO CRediT taxonomy; https://credit.niso.org/), Competing interest statements, Funding information, Data availability statements, and Supplementary information （if have） should be included at the end of the manuscript.
Supplementary Information (SI) is published online alongside the article. All SI is subject to peer review and should be cited in the main text where appropriate. Authors are expected to deposit all supplementary materials in the Science Data Bank (ScienceDB) (https://www.scidb.cn/en) under a CC BY 4.0 license as part of the submission process. A DOI or private access link must be included in the manuscript at submission to enable editorial assessment and peer review and will be published with the article. All data must be submitted within the JDIS exclusive community in ScienceDB (https://www.scidb.cn/en/c/jdis); data deposited elsewhere in ScienceDB will not be visible to the Editorial Office. Supplementary Information will be published as received, without further editing or reformatting.
How to Submit
Online submission system
JDIS uses an easily-used and user-friendly online system, ScholarOne Manuscript developed by Thomson Reuters to handle the processing of manuscripts. All manuscripts must be submitted to the system which is at: https://mc03.manuscriptcentral.com/jdis.
If you are a new user, click “Register for an account” to create an account. Once login, please follow the instructions step by step to submit your paper. If you are having trouble accessing an existing account, please email the editorial office at jdis@mail.las.ac.cn.
Preprinting
Journal of Data and Information Science is an OA journal dedicated to promoting rapid dissemination of research papers together with scholars. We encourage you to publish your manuscripts (including the initial version, revised versions, and the version accepted for publication) submitted to JDIS on a preprint server, for instance on arXiv，MetaArXiv，SocArXiv，or ChinaXiv.
ChinaXiv is a preprint platform of Chinese Academy of Science (http://www.chinaxiv.org). JDIS has established a cooperative relationship with ChinaXiv to jointly promote the manuscripts reposition, online first publication, and impact broaden of research works.
Data Sharing Policies
JDIS is committed to promoting data sharing in line with the FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable) principles and provides free data hosting and long-term preservation services for authors. Authors are required to share the data underlying their articles—particularly for data papers—by depositing it in the JDIS exclusive community in ScienceDB (https://www.scidb.cn/en/c/jdis). Any restrictions on data access must be disclosed at the time of submission.
All data must be made available to editors and peer reviewers during manuscript evaluation, and reviewers may comment on the terms of data access. Authors must include a Data Availability Statement in all manuscripts, describing where and how the data can be accessed, including links to repositories and citations of relevant datasets. Where possible, scripts and other materials used in the analysis should also be publicly archived. The Data Availability Statement should be placed at the end of the manuscript, immediately before the References section.
Confidentiality and Information Security
Authors are responsible for ensuring that their submissions comply with all applicable confidentiality, information security, and research-related disclosure requirements of their affiliated institutions and funding agencies. Manuscripts must not contain confidential or otherwise restricted information unless its disclosure has been duly authorized.
